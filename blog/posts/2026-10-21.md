# Japan Trip - Day 11

![Panorama on the way up to the top of Aso](/blog/images/asoPanoramaLine.jpg)

Heading out from Aso, to Beppu. I left the hotel in Aso a bit late, trying to sleep in to stave off the cold. I got started, and headed up the volcano to see the view. It was thankfully pretty cool today, which was a fantastic reprieve from the past few days which had been extremely hot. The top of the mountain was really cool! Unfortunately the path to view the caldera was closed, but no worry. The gift shop had multiple stickers, which were really nice, and added nicely to my helmet sticker collection.

I also explored the Volcano museum on the top of Mt. Aso, which was an interesting museum, but honestly quite dilapidated. I think it was still worth what I spent, but just not quite as high quality as what I was expecting. There were again lots of tourists around, primarily Chinese tourists this time. 

![The mountain, in the cloud line](/blog/images/asoMtClouds.JPG)

## Heading for Gas

I headed back down the mountain, in search of gasoline, as the bike was running closer to empty than I'd ever let it. I finally found a gas station, with a single bar left on the gauge, but my card kept declining. I really had to pee, so I had to ride over to a gas station, throw all of my gear on the bike, including my backpack, and rush in to use the bathroom. I didn't need anything, but I had heard that you're supposed to buy something if you use a conbini restroom, so I bought a redbull, and found a couple of waters for the trip before heading outside... IT WAS POURING. It looked a little cloudy that morning, but not like this. My backpack was on the ground too, with the wearer side facing up, so my back would be getting wet. 

I quickly pulled my rain jacket out of my duffel, in the top case, and worked to put the rain cover on my backpack. I got all of my gear together, and went back to the gas station, to get some cover from the rain. Made it there just fine, but the card reader was still giving me fuss. The attendant even had to come out to try and help, and told me that it was cash only. I only had 1000 yen on me, which was good for 5 liters. Enough to get me to the next gas station. I kept riding afterwards, since I needed to make it to Beppu, and the rain was showing no signs of stopping. 

## Cold.

My immediate thoughts were that it would be a fun experience to be riding in some light rain, and that it would let up once I got going. It did not. Once I got up to the ridge line, it continued to be cold, and continued to be wet. It started with my gloves getting wet, and my fingers feeling the moisture and pruning. The next thing to get really wet was my jeans, which just let the water right in. Following that, my boots started to feel a bit of coldness on the top of my feet, and then quickly after I felt water starting to pool up in the toe of my boot, which quickly led to the entire boot being pooled with water.

It was such a shame too, I had really hoped that I would be able to visit a bunch of cool locations today, but alas, rain rain rain rain rain rain rain rain rain rain rain.

Oh my it was so cold, and rainy, and cold, and rainy, and cold, and rainy...

It got quite hard to think about anything, and my only goal was to make it to the hotel as quickly as I could, with as few stops as possible, to take off all of my soggy gear, and warm up and be dry again. 50km. 40km. 35km. 32km. It felt like every time I checked the distance, even though the distance was decreasing, it was decreasing slower and slower. 

When I got to Beppu, I noticed it wasn't raining anymore. It was so hard to tell since everything I had with me was full of water, but when I put my foot on the ground, I could see water coming out of the air holes in the top of my boot. I even squished my toe down, and saw enough water coming out of the boot that a stream of water started to form on the ground, coming from my boot.

I did finally get to the hotel, totally safely, and immediately took everything off and hopped in the shower, set to the hottest setting. I knew, today wouldn't be good for my cold.
