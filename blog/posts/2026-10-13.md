# Japan Trip - Day 3

Honestly a great nights sleep. One thing I've noticed though, the average room temperature everywhere in Japan is a few degrees higher than the average in America. Not sure why that is, maybe just used to a hotter climate?

I left the hotel around 8:30AM. Walked around for a bit, slowly moving towards Shinbashi station, but with no urgency, since today is all about just getting to Fukuoka, so that I can pick up the bike on the 14th.

I needed some food for the trip, so I got a Tuna Mayo Onigiri, Iced Latte, and what I thought was a water. Turns out, Pocari Sweat is more akin to a gatorade, even though it looks like a clear bottle of water. Good, but not what I was expecting. I had heard that it is bad manners (or just not really the norm) to eat food while standing or walking, so I stashed the Onigiri and Pocari Sweat in my duffel, and continued on my way.

## Train Shenanigans

This being my second train, I was feeling confident that I would be able to figure out how to use one of the wall terminals on my own. I took about 3 shots at it to get it right.

- First try: Went straight to the Shinkansen booth, but realized I would still need a smaller transfer ticket first. 
- Second try: Went to the correct booth for the small train, but my card wasn't accepted. Backed off to prevent getting yelled at for being a dumb foreigner
- Third try: Grabbed a 1000 yen note out of my bag, tried same process, worked like a charm. 

Got on the train to Shinagawa station, easy. Got off at Shinagawa,and immediately found where to go for the Shinkansen. However, given my earlier frustrations with the Shinkansen machine, and how expensive the Shinkansen tickets are compared to the normal tickets, I wanted to have a human help me out with it. Found a person right away, and they very kindly helped me to figure out what I needed, and I made it onto the very next Shinkansen in the nick of time.
