# Getting Started

Welcome to my blog! I've been working on developing this website for a while now, off and on. While working on the site, and building little projects, it's been nice to write short summaries about the project, and detail a tiny bit of how it's made and what is interesting. However, when I'm not working on projects that will end up on this site, or when I've been working on a project that is just taking a while, I have wanted a place to consolidate my thoughts. I think that's what this blog is going to be.

## Goals?

I frequently see people who work on large projects, or who have public websites having publicly stated goals for what they want to achieve. I don't think I really have a goal here, other than to share the thought process and working process that I'm taking when developing GIS apps and tools that interest me. I'm not really making these apps or tools with any one particular user in mind, but having a way to publicly share the projects I've worked on is really good motivation to continue building.

## What's next?

I think over the next few weeks I'll start sharing some more of the thought process and build process for the apps and widgets that I've already built. I really enjoyed working on the [Calendar Widget](https://exb.luciuscreamer.com/calendar/), so I may do more of a dive into how that came about. Same with the [Radar Widget](https://exb.luciuscreamer.com/radartest/) that I've been working on, and have made some recent updates to. More to come in those areas I'm sure.
